# Bildprompt für Gemini – „Nūntius et canis" (AB 02, Lektion 15) · Fassung 2

**Was sich gegenüber Fassung 1 geändert hat:** Die Sprechblasen müssen im Bild
tatsächlich gezeichnet sein, und in **jedem** Feld muss ein Biss zu sehen sein –
entweder in der Szene selbst oder als kleines Bild **innerhalb** der Sprechblase.
Sonst zeigt das Bild nicht, was die Verbform bedeutet.

Die lateinischen Wörter stehen als **Bildunterschrift** unter jedem Feld, nicht in
der Sprechblase – in der Blase steht die gezeichnete Szene.

## Prompt (englisch, für Gemini / Imagen)

A six-panel black-and-white comic strip in clean, friendly line art, arranged in
3 columns and 2 rows. Each panel is framed by a thin rounded rectangle and numbered
1 to 6 in a small filled circle in the top-left corner. Bold simple outlines, white
background, no shading, no colour except a light blue accent, designed to stay
legible when photocopied. Leave an empty strip at the bottom of every panel for a
caption to be added later.

Consistent characters in every panel: a cheerful MAILMAN with a peaked cap, shoulder
bag and letters; a small floppy-eared DOG with its jaws wide open showing teeth,
clamped onto the mailman. A third character, AULUS, is a boy without a cap in a
light tunic, always pointing with a straight outstretched arm.

**In every single panel a dog must be visibly biting a mailman** — either in the
main scene, or as a small drawing inside a speech bubble. Draw the speech bubbles
as real rounded outlined bubbles with a pointed tail. The bubbles contain a small
picture, never any text.

Panel 1: the dog bites the mailman's hip; letters fly through the air; his arms fly up.
Panel 2: ONE mailman stands on the left, calm, holding a letter, and speaks. A large
speech bubble on the right contains a small drawing of that same mailman being bitten
by a dog.
Panel 3: THREE identical mailmen stand side by side at the bottom and speak together.
One large shared speech bubble above them contains a small drawing of three mailmen
each being bitten by a dog.
Panel 4: Aulus stands on the left pointing at ONE mailman on the right who is at that
moment being bitten by a dog.
Panel 5: Aulus stands on the left pointing at TWO mailmen on the right, each being
bitten by his own dog.
Panel 6: TWO mailmen, each being bitten by a dog, no Aulus, no speech bubble.

No text anywhere in the image.
