# Bildprompt für Gemini – „Nūntius et canis" (AB 02, Lektion 15)

Falls du die selbstgezeichnete Bildfolge (`nuntius_bildfolge.png`) durch eine
generierte ersetzen willst: Der folgende Prompt erzeugt dieselbe Sechserfolge.
Wichtig ist, dass Person und Numerus in jedem Feld eindeutig ablesbar bleiben –
sonst funktioniert die induktive Aufgabe nicht.

## Prompt (englisch, für Gemini / Imagen)

A six-panel black-and-white comic strip in clean, friendly line art, arranged in
3 columns and 2 rows, each panel framed by a thin rounded rectangle, numbered 1 to 6
in a small circle in the top-left corner. Simple bold outlines, white background,
no shading, no colour except a light blue accent — designed to stay legible when
photocopied. Consistent characters across all panels: a cheerful MAILMAN with a
peaked cap, shoulder bag and letters, and a small floppy-eared DOG with an open
mouth showing teeth. A second character, AULUS, is a boy without a cap wearing a
light tunic, always pointing.

Panel 1: the dog bites the mailman's hip from behind; letters fly through the air;
the mailman's arms are up in shock.
Panel 2: the mailman stands alone in the foreground complaining, the dog trots away
in the background; empty speech bubble above the mailman.
Panel 3: three identical mailmen stand side by side complaining together; one shared
empty speech bubble above them.
Panel 4: Aulus stands on the left pointing at ONE mailman on the right who is being
bitten by the dog; empty speech bubble above Aulus.
Panel 5: Aulus stands on the left pointing at TWO mailmen on the right; empty speech
bubble above Aulus.
Panel 6: two dogs bite two mailmen at the same time; no speech bubble.

Leave every speech bubble empty — the Latin words are added afterwards.
No text anywhere in the image.
